# Static Resonance

Origins add-on for **Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge**. One origin: five electrostatic modes, Charge 0–100, wall climb, and a few passives.

This is the Origin power of **Pipothai's own character**. Shared here so anyone who likes it can use it.

By **Pipothai** · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Tuna_Sandwich_TOT)

## Play

Hold **R** = skill wheel (select only). **F** = cast.

- **Dust** — Dust Curtain: burst 5 / zone 10
- **Sense** — Static Scan: 8. Private lock corners (green / white / red)
- **Control** — Magnetic Lock: 15 on hit / Field 40
- **Aegis** — Faraday Cage: 50 · 30s · 120s cooldown. Emergency raise is free
- **Circuit** — F toggles one redstone piece for 8s

Particles are white charge / lightning. Faraday Cage FX is unchanged.

## Passives

- **Static Footing** — knockback resist
- **Charged Stability** — extra resist at Charge 50+
- **Wall climb** — free
- **Faraday Protocol** — cage can raise for free if a hit would drop you below 2 HP (Aegis sub-panel)
- **Grounded** — water/rain leak Charge; +25% damage while wet

## Need

Minecraft **1.20.1** · **Forge 47.x** · **[Origins (Forge)](https://modrinth.com/mod/origins-forge) 1.10.x** · this mod on **client and server**

Optional: [Cloth Config](https://modrinth.com/mod/cloth-config) for in-game config.

Scan see-through is **off** by default (`visuals.seeThroughWalls`). Not a cheat client.
